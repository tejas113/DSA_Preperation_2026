# G. Questions to ask the interviewer (last 5 min of every round)

Have **2–3 genuine** questions per round. Don't ask about salary, perks, or anything Google's careers page answers.
Pick 2–3 for your round.

**Good ones**
- What's something you learned at Google that surprised you, compared to where you worked before?
- What does a new L3 engineer's first 6 months look like on your team? What separates the ones who ramp up fastest?
- How does your team handle disagreements on technical direction?
- What's the most interesting problem you've worked on recently?
- How do you personally get feedback on your work here?
- What do you wish you'd known when you joined?
