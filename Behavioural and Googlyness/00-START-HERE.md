## Part 2 – One method for all 24 "what would you do" questions (F1–F24)
1. **Understand**: clarify, list the relevant factors, find the **root cause** (not just the symptom)
2. **Gather info**: what would I check or ask? Have I faced something similar?
3. **Options**: 2–3 possible approaches with **pros and cons**
4. **Take a stand**: pick one and say why it beats the others despite its cons
5. **Success metric**: how I'd know it worked
6. **Stay open**: "If X turned out to be true, I'd switch to Y"

For people problems, always go **private conversation first, manager last.**
(Built from Google's official careers guidance plus the ex-Google HM's "take a stand" advice.)
